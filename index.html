Chronologe

For people who think about time.

Chronologe is a personal time tracker for iPhone and iPad, built for people who care about the exact distance between moments.

Add Events for the moments in your life that matter, and see exactly how long ago or how far away they are, in the units you choose.

Create Countdowns for things in progress: set a duration and let Chronologe tick down to zero, with a Live Activity on your Lock Screen.

Use the Calculator to compare two dates, or to find the date you arrive at after adding or subtracting any amount of time.

Your data stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud. Chronologe has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.
