Getting Started

A quick tour of Chronologe.

Chronologe has three tabs: Events, Countdowns, and the Calculator. Each one also has its own help page in Settings.

Add an event

On the Events tab, tap + and give the event a title and a date. It can be in the past or the future. Choose the units you want to see: years, months, weeks, days (or business days), hours, minutes, and seconds. Turn off Include Time for birthdays and anniversaries, where only the date matters.

The list shows how long ago or how far away each event is, and keeps it up to date. To edit or delete an event, swipe left on it, or touch and hold it.

Tap an event

Tapping an event opens its Occurrences sheet. At the top is the time between the event and right now. Tap that value to change its units: a row of unit pills appears, and tapping a pill turns that unit on or off. To use the new units in the list and in reminders too, tap Use in List.

Below that, step through the event's past and future occurrences, yearly, monthly, or weekly. Each one shows how far away it is and the date it falls on. On iPad, the occurrences appear beside the list instead of in a sheet.

Run a countdown

A countdown is a timer you control. On the Countdowns tab, tap +, set a duration, and tap Start. Tap a countdown to see it as a large clock, with Pause and Reset.

While it runs, the countdown appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. When it reaches zero, Chronologe alerts you, even with a Focus on. iOS limits a Live Activity to about 8 hours, so a longer countdown shows one once you open the app with 8 hours or less to go.

Calculate with dates

The Calculator measures the time between two dates, or finds the date you reach by adding or subtracting time. Either date can come from one of your events: tap Choose from Events. In Offset mode, you can save the result as a new event.

Your data

Your events, countdowns, and categories sync through iCloud to every device signed in to the same Apple Account. A running countdown stays on the device where you started it, so it only alerts you there.

To keep a backup, use Settings → Export Data. Support has more about syncing and backups.
