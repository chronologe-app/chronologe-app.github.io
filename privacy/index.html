Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Chronologe doesn't collect, store, or share any of your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

Your data stays yours

The events, countdowns, categories, and settings you create are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, Chronologe uses Apple's iCloud to keep them in sync across your devices. That data is kept in your own private iCloud storage, under your Apple Account. The developer can't see or access it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications and Live Activities

Reminders, countdown alerts, and Live Activities are created on your device. Nothing is sent to a server run by the developer.

Exporting and sharing

When you use Export Data or Share, Chronologe creates a file or text and hands it to the place you choose. What happens to it after that depends on where you send it.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may give the developer anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Chronologe doesn't add any of its own.

Beta testing

If you test a beta version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares some information with the developer, such as your name, email address, crash reports, and any feedback you send. This is covered by Apple's TestFlight terms, not by Chronologe.

Children

Chronologe doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@chronologe.app
