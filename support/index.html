Support

Questions, problems, or ideas? Email support@chronologe.app. It helps to include your device, its iOS version, and the version of Chronologe (Settings → About Chronologe).

Help in the app

Settings has a help page for Events, Countdowns, and the Calculator.

Syncing between devices

Chronologe syncs your events, countdowns, and categories through iCloud when you're signed in to the same Apple Account on each device. If you sign out of iCloud, or turn off iCloud for Chronologe, iOS removes them from that device until you turn it back on.

A running countdown stays on the device where you started it, so it only alerts there. Your other devices show the countdown as not started, and can run it on their own.

Backing up your data

Settings → Export Data saves everything to a file you can keep in Files or anywhere else. Import Data restores it, replacing what's on the device.
